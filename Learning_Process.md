# Learning Process

## Q1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method by which we can know where we understand a concept deeply and explain it in simple words as if we are teaching someone else or teaching to a kid.

## Q2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

## Q3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Active Mode:** When we focus completely on a task or problem.

**Diffused Mode:** When we relax and our brain keeps thinking and learning, like when we walk or take a break.

## Q4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.

- **Choose a skill to learn:** First, decide what you want to learn and why you want to learn it.

- **Break the skill into smaller parts:** Divide the topic into small and easy parts so that it becomes easier to understand and learn.

- **Learn enough to start practicing:** Watch videos, read articles, or study basic concepts to understand the topic before starting practical work.

- **Remove distractions and practice:** Keep your phone away and focus on learning. Try to practice the skill for at least 20 hours to improve your understanding and confidence.

- **Focus on practice:** Practice regularly, learn from your mistakes, and try to improve your skills with every attempt.
