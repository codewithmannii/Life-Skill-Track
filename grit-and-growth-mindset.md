# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

Grit means having passion and staying committed to our long-term goals, even when things become difficult. Success is not only about talent or intelligence but also about working hard, patience and consistency.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and abilities through learning, practice and hard work. Instead of being afraid of mistakes, we should accept challenges and learn from them.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions and efforts have an impact on our results. 
The key point of the video is that instead of blaming situations or other people, we should focus on the things we can control and take responsibility for our own progress and try to continously look towards what is comming next.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

Key points to build a growth mindset->
1. Believe in your ability to learn new things.
2. Accept challenges and difficulties with a smile on your face.
3. Learn from your mistakes and failures.
4. Be open to feedback from anyone.
5. Focus on improvement rather than perfection.
6. Be patient and consistent.
7. Step out of your comfort zone and try harder.
8. Never stop learning.
9. Believe that you can figure things out.
10. Appreciate the learning process and enjoy it.

## 5. Mindset - A MountBlue Warrior Reference Manual

### Question 5

My ideas to build a growth mindset as a software developer->
1. Practice coding every day and try to solve new problems.
2. Don't give up when I get stuck on a difficult problem.
3. Learn from my mistakes instead of repeating them.
4. Take feedback from mentors and teammates positively.
5. Try to understand the concepts instead of just memorizing code.
6. Explore new technologies and keep improving my technical skills.
7. Ask questions whenever I don't understand something.
8. Learn from other developers and try to understand their approaches.
9. Take responsibility for my tasks and mistakes.
10. Set small daily goals and track my progress.
11. Work on improving my weak areas instead of avoiding them.
12. Stay consistent and never stop learning.

I believe that becoming a good software developer takes time, practice and patience. I will focus on improving a little every day rather than expecting instant results.
