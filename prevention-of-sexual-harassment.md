# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour that makes someone feel uncomfortable or unsafe at the workplace.

- Making inappropriate comments about someone's body or appearance.
- Passing sexual jokes, suggestive remarks, or offensive comments.
- Unwanted touching, staring, whistling, or making inappropriate gestures.
- Sending inappropriate messages, emails, images, or videos.
- Repeatedly asking someone for dates even after they refuse.
- Treating someone unfairly or making gender-based comments.

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness sexual harassment, I would take it seriously and follow these steps:

1. Ask the person to stop if I feel safe doing so.
2. Keep a record of the incident, including dates, times, and possible witnesses.
3. Report the incident to the company's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
4. Support the affected person and encourage them to report the incident.
5. Maintain confidentiality and cooperate with any investigation.

I believe everyone deserves a safe and respectful workplace, and we should never ignore such behaviour.
